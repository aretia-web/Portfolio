01 · About

Rigorous thinking, shipped as real products.

I'm a software developer from Sevilla. I like problems that sit at the intersection of rigorous theory and practical results — and turning them into things people actually use.

Today I'm the co-founder and CTO of Deskly: a secure server we install inside each company that connects their ERP, CRM and any other tool, so teams can query data and run tasks by chat or voice — without their data ever leaving their network.

I also do algorithm development at the Club de Algoritmia, worked on LiDAR perception for the ARUS autonomous racing team, and co-founded Microweb, a small studio building landing pages for local businesses.
